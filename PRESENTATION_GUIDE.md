# Staff review demonstration

A short walkthrough for a presentation or handoff:

1. **Overview:** explain that the app ranks apparent need, not proven impact.
   Filter to a school type and grade level; change the number of schools shown.
   Use service status to distinguish existing service from potential expansion.
2. **School Explorer:** open a school, show how its current weighted component
   contributions add up to its scenario score, then show named nearby food sites
   and their provider references.
3. **Distance review:** select a service group and school type. Enter a minimum
   school separation, explain the green/gray recommendations, and inspect a
   pair's rubric comparison. Distances are straight-line and the Share pin is
   approximate; this is not a delivery-route optimizer.
4. **Budget Lab:** enter a budget, school cost, capacity, and participation rate.
   Compare the three strategies and download the comparison or a portfolio.
   Switch to the outcomes tab to explain the distinction between estimated reach
   and observed results.
5. **Handoff:** download the Overview review brief for the chosen shortlist.
   Point readers to Methodology & Sources and the original master workbook.

## State the evidence limits clearly

The core data snapshot is October 9, 2026. Food-site details are saved listings,
not real-time availability. Fresh-access scores currently use listed USDA
supermarkets/superstores; verified broader produce-outlet data is still pending.
Service status comes from the supplied list and requires reconciliation before
commitments. Outcome logs need CSV download to survive the session. Before/after
survey changes alone are not causal effects.

The review refinements preserve the source school data and rubric. They add
service-status visibility, clear/reset controls, reproducible shortlist exports,
more useful empty states, and independent outcome recording.
